How to use Manico

Import a track, inspect the bass line on the fretboard, correct the transcription and practise every passage. Audio and edits stay in your browser.

Getting startedPracticeEditingPlay alongExportTroubleshooting

1. Import and transcribe

  1. Press Import audio or drop a file on the home page.
  2. Choose MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG or FLAC.
  3. Adjust attack sensitivity: increase it when notes are missing, reduce it when too many appear.
  4. Press Transcribe and save.

Isolate the bass with AI

Leave Isolate the bass with AI ticked and Manico first separates the bass from the rest of the recording, then transcribes the bass alone: far fewer wrong notes, especially on dense mixes. The first time it downloads a 174 MB model, which stays in the browser; a track takes a few minutes, less with a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer with a graphics card. A track imported earlier can be separated later with Isolate the bass (AI) in the Practice panel.

Without separation, use a clean mix with an audible bass and limited overlap from the kick drum or other low instruments.

2. Practise a passage

  • Play/Pause starts and stops the track.
  • The timeline changes the listening position.
  • The tablature above the fretboard scrolls in time: the number under the yellow line is the note to play now, and the fretboard shows only that one. Tapping an empty spot on the tablature moves there.
  • The tablature is divided into numbered bars. Under the staff, stems, beams and flags show the length: a plain stem is a quarter, one beam eighths, two beams sixteenths, a short stem a half note, a dot adds half as much again. A held note is written once: an arc ties it to where it carries on, and in a new bar the fret is repeated in brackets.
  • In the Practice panel, Bars corrects what only the ear can decide: move the bar lines by a beat, double or halve the tempo, switch between 4/4 and 3/4.
  • Transcribe the notes again reads the track again with the sensitivity you choose: higher if repeated notes are missing, lower if a held note is broken up.
  • Speed slows down to 50% while preserving pitch.
  • Listen to, on tracks with an isolated bass, switches between the full track, the track without bass to play over the real band, and the bass only.
  • Set A/B creates a loop; both markers appear on the timeline.
  • Tuning, fret count and look-ahead notes live in the Practice panel.

Keyboard shortcuts

Space play/pause · ←/→ previous/next note · [ point A · ] point B.

3. Correct the transcription

Select a note by tapping its number in the tablature above the fretboard. In Correct note you can:

  • move by a semitone or select the exact pitch;
  • lock a string and fret;
  • edit start and end times;
  • split, merge or delete the note;
  • add a note at the cursor.

Fingering is recalculated automatically while locked positions remain unchanged.

4. Play along

  1. Open a track or included exercise.
  2. Connect your instrument and preferably use headphones.
  3. Press Start microphone and grant browser access.
  4. Play the highlighted note: Manico shows the detected note, timing and score.
The microphone is analysed in memory: Manico never records or uploads it. With open speakers, the track itself may interfere with detection.

5. Export

  • TAB: readable text tablature.
  • MIDI: Type 0 file for DAWs and notation software.
  • Project: JSON containing notes, timing, positions and settings.

The original audio is not included in exports.

6. Common issues

The transcription has errors

Try a different sensitivity, then correct doubtful points in the editor. Dense mixes and overlapping instruments can confuse any monophonic detector.

The microphone will not start

Check site permissions, use HTTPS and make sure another application is not holding the input.

A saved track disappeared

Data is local to the browser. Avoid clearing site data and download the project as a backup.

Privacy and compatibility

Manico works locally in the browser: audio files, transcriptions and microphone input are not uploaded. The bass separation also runs in your browser: only the model is downloaded, nothing is sent. For the best experience use a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Available features may depend on the audio formats supported by your device.
